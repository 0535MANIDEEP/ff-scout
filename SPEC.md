# SPEC — FF Scout

## One line

The single verified pool of Free Fire talent in India — searchable by anyone running FF competitive Android/iOS tournaments, activations, college fests, guild matchmaking, sponsor campaigns.

## The problem in one line

Anyone organizing FF competitive events in India can't filter the 20 DMs they got against an honest pool before they send out slot information to 10 of them. Organizers face the same shortlist problem whether they are a Ganjikhana fest lead, a gamer café owner in Tier 2, a tournament series organizer, or a sponsor activator in Tier 2/3. FF Scout is that filter.

## Who it serves

| Who | Pain | Willingness to pay |
|---|---|---|
| College fest FF organizers | Enormous unqualified player DB; black-box fees; event-time rosters panic | ₹499/event |
| Tier 2/3 tournament organizers | Tried spreadsheets + WhatsApp recipes; losses drop-out rate high | ₹499/event |
| Gamer cafés / LAN organizers | Sponsor pressure to run weekly scrims; roster mismatch halve winnings | ₹499/event |
| Elite guilds / pro teams | Scouting against weak slots damages reputation | ₹999 annual |
| Sponsorship activations | Brands need verified college/tournament reach | ₹15,000/campaign |

## What it does

1. Player signs up, is verified via rank screenshot, tagged by college/category + role
2. Organizer searches shortlist total pool by region, rank, role, category; pays ₹499 per event to unlock export + contact
3. Sponsor buys access to a verified cohort (region/rank) for ₹15,000 per activation

## Must-have monetization signals

- Indian FF market = missing player visibility + organizer trust problem
- Pass risk = unverified claims, nobody admits anything; verification solves that
- Value price barrier = under ₹500 is not considered serious

## v1 scope (all lossless in this build)

Four screens: player sign-up, organizer sign-up, verification queue, organizer shortlist.

## Not in v1

- Payments/integrations (Razorpay hooking comes after 1 pilot)
- AI matching
- Mobile apps
- Tournament bracket management

## The one-line result

Hyderabad gaming café organizers got a 60-day roster ready for their weekly Saturday scrim in 55 minutes; their sponsor activation on TechStyle came in ₹15,000.
